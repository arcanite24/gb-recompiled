Status: verified

renderer-independent, completed-frame snapshot. It carries bounded semantic

semantic mapping, conservative fallback rules, and battle-accessor boundary

## Contract

`gbrt_presentation_source_init` validates ABI v1, expected ROM size and
SHA-256, the live embedded ROM bytes, and initialized PPU/VRAM/OAM/I/O state.
An unknown ABI or ROM never produces an active source.

`gbrt_presentation_capture` runs only with `frame_done` set and validates:

- a stable scene ID and scene kind;
- up to nine bounded semantic map regions and 4,096 owned block IDs;
- required overworld or battle state;
- port UI ABI, canvas, and bounded command count; and
- the source's still-matching exact ROM.

The owned output copies:

- both CGB banks of tile data;
- both 32-by-32 tile-ID maps and corresponding bank-1 attributes;
- all 40 OAM entries with decoded coordinates, size, bank, palette, flips,
  and priority;
- the renderer-independent port command frame;
- completed-frame/cycle/PPU endpoint timing;
- LCD registers, CGB palettes, and accurate 15-bit pixels; and
- the game-specific semantic map/battle scene.

The snapshot declares `GB_PRESENTATION_SHADOW_ACCURATE_PPU` and
`guest_writeback_allowed = false`. Mutating the copied frame cannot change
VRAM, OAM, semantic scene input, registers, interrupts, saves, or the accurate
framebuffer.

## Hardware authority

The contract was checked against the pinned `tech_docs/pan_docs.md` before
implementation. Pan Docs is unambiguous for the boundary used here: two CGB
VRAM banks, 384 tiles per bank, two 32-by-32 maps, bank-1 attributes, 40 OAM
objects with a ten-object scanline limit, and 154-by-456-dot frame timing with
variable mode 3. SameBoy was not needed to resolve an ambiguity.

Native output is host presentation only. The ordinary PPU continues to own
every dot, LCDC/STAT/LY, interrupts, VRAM/OAM/palette CPU visibility, OAM DMA,
HDMA, sprite selection and priority, raster effects, and accurate pixels.
The completed-frame VRAM/OAM endpoint cannot reconstruct every raster effect,
so unmodeled Crystal scenes must retain the included accurate result.

## Limits

- ABI v1 is a snapshot/source contract, not yet a widescreen renderer.
- Crystal map block/layout, event-object, and occlusion accessors remain M7-002.
- Typed current-battle semantic accessors and replacement assets remain
  M7-003.
- The snapshot is copied at completed-frame boundaries. It deliberately does
  not promise mid-frame native raster reconstruction.
- This proves the established route and contract behavior, not whole-game
  compatibility or GPU/platform performance.
