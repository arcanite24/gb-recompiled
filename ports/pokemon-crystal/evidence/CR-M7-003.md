## Contract

`GBPresentationReplacementConfig` carries original/native mode, exact ROM,
output dimensions, deterministic seed, and up to 64 replacement assets.
`gbrt_presentation_validate_replacements` rejects unknown ABI or ROM,
unsupported dimensions/mode, native mode without assets, malformed content
hashes, missing license/source, and duplicate IDs. The contract contains no
SDL, GPU, texture, or graphics-API type.

Crystal's typed reader uses the live battle structure when available. At the
locked battle-intro boundary it safely resolves the reviewed active party slot
into the party record, then reads the enemy and battle context. Invalid mode,
slot, species, level, ROM, or bounds prevents native scene creation.

The checked asset package supplies `crystal.ui.panel-v1` and
`crystal.battle.aura-v1`. Both are JSON-authored procedural descriptions,
licensed `CC0-1.0`, sourced to the Crystal Recompiled project, and verified by
content hash before the probe renders.
