# CR-M8-008 — Clean-package integrated acceptance

Status: accepted for the initial alpha scope on 2026-08-02. Automated gates are
complete; the combined physical-controller walkthrough is explicitly deferred
as post-alpha usability work.

## Outcome

The macOS arm64 BYOR package reconstructs the exact supported Crystal ROM into
a private generated project, builds it with the Challenge native patch, and
passes both the retained vanilla route and the deterministic Challenge route.
The packaged executable also passes the controller-panel lifecycle, data-mod
loading and vanilla recovery, save preservation, and a separate first-run
privacy audit.

Automated route evidence is authoritative for exact guest state and determinism.
The release owner accepted that evidence for the initial fan-project alpha and
deferred a physical controller user's combined enable, visible wild/trainer,
disable, and restart walkthrough. That deferred check remains usability evidence
and is not represented as having passed.

  IDs, generated metadata, runtime contracts, and the seven replay-preflight
  negative controls in CR-M8-001, CR-M8-002, CR-M8-004, and CR-M8-006.
- **M8-R2 determinism:** proven by the three-run enabled route and complete ROM,
  executable, receipt, ruleset, configuration, mods, seed, and input identity
  projection in CR-M8-006.
- **M8-R3 bounded mutation:** proven by synthetic atomic transaction tests,
  failure injection, one-byte dirty-range evidence, reviewed wild/trainer
  guards, and coherent derived battle state in CR-M8-001 through CR-M8-003.
- **M8-R4 vanilla equivalence:** proven by disabled/removed full routes and the
  save, restart, Continue, removal, reinstall, and malformed-configuration
  lifecycle in CR-M8-007.
- **M8-R5 native usability:** automated panel state, input capture,
  explanation, Apply/cancel/disable, restart, headless, and package tests pass;
  physical controller comprehension and visible-results UAT is deferred for the
  initial alpha.
- **M8-R6 reusable recompiler value:** gameplay-mutation ABI v1 and host
  configuration ABI v1 are game-agnostic, documented, synthetic-fixture
  tested, emitted through generated metadata, and consumed by Crystal policy.
- **M8-R7 legal boundary:** the verified alpha source/package/archive inventories and
  strengthened privacy verifier prove ROM-free, generated-game-free,

The audit leaves only the manual portion of M8-R5 unproven. The release owner

the automated or physical-UAT claims.

## Deferred physical boundary

Package-native, audio-compatible pre-wild and pre-trainer checkpoints were
created from the proven Challenge route solely to shorten manual UAT. The first
interactive load preflight passed. They are private ignored artifacts and do
not replace the clean-start route proof.

The 8BitDo controller is not currently exposed by macOS Bluetooth or HID, so no
combined physical input or visual-result attestation has been accepted. That
walkthrough remains tracked as post-alpha usability work and must be completed
before claiming physical Challenge Mode acceptance.
