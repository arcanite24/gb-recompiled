# CR-M8-002 — Trainer-battle mutation tracer

Status: verified

Date: 2026-08-01

## Result

PASS. The exact-ROM `TryAddMonToParty` function now accepts a typed
`crystal.trainer-party-level.v1` pre-hook event through the reusable gameplay
mutation ABI. On the retained early-trainer route, vanilla constructed a level
5 Cyndaquil and the tracer constructed a level 8 Cyndaquil. The generated
original body then derived every reviewed party statistic coherently.

The hook is constrained to opposing trainer-party construction: normal and
can-lose trainer battles are accepted only when a trainer class is present,
the low `wMonType` nibble is `OTPARTYMON`, link mode is off, Battle Tower mode
is off, the opponent party has room, and the proposed level is valid. Player,
caught, gift, trade, link, Battle Tower, full-party, unsupported battle-type,
and invalid-level fixtures perform no transaction.

## Investigative failures retained

- `final-trainer-proof-20260801` used both fixed-offset tracers. The earlier
  stronger wild encounter changed the cycle-anchored route before the trainer
  checkpoint and exposed one unrelated fallback at `041:60C7`. This is an
  input-routing issue to solve for the integrated S06 route, not trainer-hook
  evidence.
- `final-trainer-only-proof-20260801` omitted the return-stack contract and
  correctly failed before mutation because the `predef` entry was not a
  generated `CALL/RST` contract.
- `final-trainer-only-proof2-20260801` proved the entry contract and zero
  fallback but did not mutate because the initial guard assumed trainer battle
  mode was already set. Source and route evidence show the party is constructed
  earlier, so the accepted guard uses the trainer class, party destination,
  battle type, link, Battle Tower, and capacity semantics instead.
- `final-trainer-only-proof3-20260801` produced the correct coherent trainer
  but exposed an over-strong equality check on post-battle persistence bytes.
  The final verifier retains hashes and enforces unchanged file layout; full
  disabled/removal byte equivalence remains a later milestone gate.

## Limits

- This slice deliberately uses a fixed +3 tracer. The shared party/badge-aware
  `challenge-v1` calculation begins in CR-M8-003.
- The retained trainer replay proves one type-1 party with one opponent. The
  guard matrix covers the excluded construction modes synthetically; broader
  route coverage remains future compatibility work.
- The combined wild-and-trainer replay needs a behavior-aware input route
  rather than reusing a cycle schedule captured from vanilla gameplay. That is
  part of CR-M8-006 deterministic-route acceptance.
