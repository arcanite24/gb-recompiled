The parser now retains constants separately from address symbols. It reports
address records, unique addresses, duplicate-address records, constant
records, unique constants, and duplicate identical constants. Conflicting
constant definitions and malformed or out-of-range records fail closed and
clear partial parser state.

## Gate audit

- **Complete syntax:** banked address records and constant-only hexadecimal
  records are parsed deliberately. Constants have a separate typed API and
  cannot become ROM functions merely by being present.
- **Focused coverage:** the repository test covers identical and conflicting
  constants, duplicate-address aliases, full-line and trailing comments,
  fixed and banked RAM, malformed record shapes, malformed numbers, address
  bounds, and a constant wider than 32 bits.
- **Direct raw import:** both the default probe and default Crystal generator
  consume the locked raw `pokecrystal11.sym`; `prepare_symbols.py` is no
  longer required for compatibility.
- **Expected counts:** the live probe reports exactly 58,456 address records,
  53,784 unique addresses, 4,672 duplicate-address aliases, 164 constant
  records, and 164 unique constants.
- **Generated behavior:** a fresh raw-symbol project generated, configured,
  built, and produced a headless state dump. Raw input generated the same
  code and metadata as the historical address-only projection.
- **Artifact boundary:** the ROM, pinned upstream symbol file, generated
  projects, persistence files, and raw logs remain ignored.

## Limits

- Constants are modeled by the parser but are not yet exported as semantic
  memory-space metadata; that is CR-M3-003.
- Duplicate-address aliases are counted, while one deterministic canonical
  `Symbol` remains address-indexed. Preserving all rich aliases independently
  of analyzer trust belongs to CR-M3-002.
- This item proves parser compatibility and unchanged generated output. It
  does not claim that imported address names are trustworthy function or data
  boundaries; CR-M3-002 exists to separate those policies.
- The 121-frame smoke proves fresh generated behavior for this parser change,
  not the complete gameplay route or broad Crystal compatibility.
